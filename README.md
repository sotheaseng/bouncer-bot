# 🚪 Bouncer Bot

You're the bouncer. You decide who gets in — and who doesn't.

Bouncer Bot is a fast-paced decision game where a queue of characters lines up at your door. Some are clean. Some are infected. You've got a split second to read the situation and make the call: **Allow** or **Reject**. Let the queue pile up, lose a life, let in the wrong one — game over.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

- Characters spawn and line up in the queue
- The character at the front is waiting on **your** call
- Hit **Allow** to let them in, **Reject** to turn them away
- **Infected characters** have a red marker — reject them
- Let the queue grow past 10 and you lose a life
- You've got **3 lives** — make them count
- Clear **25 characters** to advance to the next level
- Each level the queue moves faster — stay sharp

---

## ✨ Features

- Sprite-based characters with randomized clothing
- Infection system with clear visual indicators
- Smooth queue animations
- Progressive difficulty scaling
- Lives system and level progression
- Looping background music
- Android (APK) support
